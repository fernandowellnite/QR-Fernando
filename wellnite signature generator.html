This link is available in the last column of the spreadsheet called "Signature QR".
How to use it:
1. Fill in your info above (the signature below updates automatically).
2. Click "Copy signature".
3. Go to Gmail → gear icon ⚙️ → See all settings → General tab → Signature.
4. Click inside the signature box and paste (Ctrl/Cmd+V).
5. Scroll down and click "Save changes".

If the "Copy signature" button doesn't work in your browser: click inside the signature box below, select all (Ctrl/Cmd+A), and copy manually (Ctrl/Cmd+C).
